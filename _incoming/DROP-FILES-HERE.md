# Drop files here

Dump the STLs and the F3D into this folder. Do not rename, sort, or tidy anything.

Whatever the slicer or Fusion called them is fine. Duplicates are fine. Extra parts that
turn out not to belong are fine.

Claude sorts them into `cad/` and `stl/`, names them against the size table in the README,
and deletes this folder in the same commit.

If a filename is the only thing that records which flute or which bore a part was for,
say so rather than assuming it survives the rename.
